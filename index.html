How it sounds

The sounds, one by one

Everything is made live in your browser. No samples, no recordings. The style is acid techno, after the Roland TB-303 basslines of late-80s Chicago.

SoundHow it is madeWhat you hear
BasslineEach letter's drawing is looped hundreds of times a second as one vibration.The shape is the tone: curves sound soft, corners buzz.
PitchFaster loop, higher note. Letters climb the A minor scale; numbers are scale steps.Any text sounds in key.
SquelchA resonant filter opens and closes on every note. The Filter slider sets how far.The wet "acid" sound.
Accent · slideTall letters hit harder; vowels glide from the previous note.The classic 303 bite and bend.
KickA low tone that drops quickly in pitch, on every beat.Four-on-the-floor thump.
Hi-hatShort burst of filtered noise between the beats.The tss on the offbeat.
ClapMid-range noise burst on beats 2 and 4.The snap that keeps time.
LoopOne character per 16th note at 128 BPM. The beat repeats every 16 steps.Text that is not 16 long drifts against the beat, so the loop keeps shifting.

Rule 2

Letter families

Like type designers, we start from n and o and build the rest from them.

n m h r u

Arches. m is two n's, u is n upside down.

o c e s

Rounds. c and e are open o's.

b d p q

Mirrors and flips of one shape.

a g

Bowl plus hook.

l i j t f

Stems. i and j share the dot.

v w x y k z

Diagonals. w is two v's.

Rule 3

From shape to sound

Height → octave

Tall letters b d f h k l t go up an octave. Tail letters g j p q y go down into the sub bass.

Size → volume

Bigger shapes play louder.

Stroke → tone

Curves are warm. Straight lines buzz.

Width → length

Narrow i j l f t r are short. Wide m w are long.

Vowels → slide

a e i o u glide in from the note before.

Dot · double → rhythm

The dot adds a quick second hit. Double letters hold as one note.

Numbers

0 to 9

Oldstyle figures

0 1 2 sit at x-height, 6 8 rise, 3 4 5 7 9 drop, just like lowercase.

One width

All digits share one width, so they all play the same length.

Scale steps

The digit is the note: 1 A, 2 B, 3 C … 8 A an octave up. 0 is the low A.
